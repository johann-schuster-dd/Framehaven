# Framehaven

Create beautiful fullscreen slideshows from your favorite photos and music.

## What it does

Framehaven turns photo collections into personal slideshows on your Mac. Create a slideshow, add pictures from folders or Photos—including a complete Photos album chosen from its folder hierarchy—and choose how they should be presented.

You can arrange pictures by file name or date, shuffle them, adjust the time per picture, enable the Ken Burns effect, and repeat the slideshow in an endless loop. Add a soundtrack using audio files from Finder, a playlist, or an album from your Music library, with optional song shuffle.

During playback, Framehaven provides controls for pausing, moving between pictures, closing the slideshow, and moving it to another connected display. To show the complete slideshow on an Apple TV, open Screen Mirroring in the macOS menu bar and select your TV. Framehaven follows a newly connected extended display automatically.

On first launch, a short welcome screen explains how to get started. Framehaven Settings contains the privacy policy, this repository, and the contact address.

Framehaven shows album counting progress while opening the Photos album picker, and progress while it imports Photos content or prepares pictures. The cycle duration is displayed beside the Play button so you can see the complete running time before playback begins.
